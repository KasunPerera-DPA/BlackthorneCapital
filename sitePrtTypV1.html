About Blackthorne

Independent capital.
Long-term perspective.

Blackthorne Capital Group is being established as a private investment company focused on disciplined capital deployment. We seek opportunities where commercial insight, thoughtful structuring and strong relationships can contribute to enduring value.

Our approach is grounded in integrity, independent judgement and an appreciation of both opportunity and risk.

Identify relevant opportunities through direct introductions and professional networks.
